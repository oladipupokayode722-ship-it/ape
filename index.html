    FAQ

    Questions.

    What is Ink Genesis Apes?

    A hand-drawn Ape PFP collection of 2,222 Apes. It's the first Ape collection on Ink.

    When is the mint and how much is it?

    The mint date and price haven't been announced yet. Announcements go out on X first: @InkGenesisApes.

    How do I get on the whitelist?

    Complete the X tasks on the whitelist page (follow us, then like and repost our first post), then submit your X handle, wallet address and repost link.

    Which wallet should I use?

    Any wallet that supports Ink. Your address starts with 0x and is 42 characters long.

    What do holders get?

    Access to the Ape Den: OG status, rewards, early access, future mint priority, partner perks and Ape Missions.
